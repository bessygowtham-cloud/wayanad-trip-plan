Right place, right time: your overnight drive puts you near Gundlupet around the morning safari slot (6:00–9:30 AM). Route to Masinagudi

Via Sulthan Bathery (24 km) → Gudalur (44.2 km more) → Masinagudi (25.3 km more). This crosses the Mudumalai forest stretch, which (like Bandipur) is closed to all vehicles 9 PM–6 AM — the same rule on both the Mysuru and Masinagudi roads, since it's one contiguous reserve. At ~3h total, there's room to leave well before dark; depart by 4 PM to be safely checked in before the closure, earlier if you want daylight at the resort.
